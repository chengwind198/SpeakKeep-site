Privacy Policy

SpeakKeep has no developer server and no account system. Recordings, transcripts, tags and tasks stay on your device unless you explicitly turn on iCloud private sync.

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · applies to SpeakKeep 1.0.1 and later.

What we collect

We do not collect personal data. SpeakKeep does not require an account, does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not upload your recordings, transcripts, tags, tasks or device identifiers to a developer server. SpeakKeep cannot read another app’s recordings; imports only happen when you choose a file through the system share sheet or file picker.

Where your data is stored

DataLocation
Recordings (.m4a)App sandbox Documents (visible in the Files app)
Notes, transcripts, timestamps, tags, tasksApp sandbox database
Full-text search indexApp sandbox support directory (rebuildable)
Spotlight indexYour device; can be cleared in Settings
Local transcription modelApp sandbox; a static file that does not include your content

Deleting a note removes its audio and related data. Deleting the app removes all data stored on that device.

iCloud private sync (optional, included with the one-time purchase)

iCloud sync is off by default and only starts after you turn it on in Settings → iCloud Sync. When enabled, note text syncs to your own private CloudKit database and recordings are stored in the SpeakKeep folder inside your own iCloud Drive. Both live in your iCloud account; the developer cannot read them. Turning the switch off and reopening the app stops syncing; local data is not deleted. On a new device, text arrives first and audio downloads on demand.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only while you are recording a voice note.
  • Speech recognition — converts recordings to text. SpeakKeep uses on-device recognition and does not fall back to network recognition.
  • Photos (add only) — declared for saving a share card image; it is not requested until you use that action.

You can change any permission in iOS Settings. Existing data is not affected.

Network access

SpeakKeep makes network requests only to:

  1. initialize the optional local transcription model (a static file);
  2. complete App Store purchases and restore purchases through StoreKit.

Transcription, search, tagging and task extraction run on device and do not need a network connection.

Children

SpeakKeep does not collect data from anyone, including children. The app is rated 4+.

Contact

Questions about this policy: visit the support page.

隐私政策（简体中文）

最后更新：2026-10-17，适用于 SpeakKeep 1.0.1 及以后版本。

一句话说明

SpeakKeep 没有开发者服务器，也没有账号体系。录音、转写文本、标签与任务默认全部在你的设备上处理与保存；只有你主动开启 iCloud 私有同步时，数据才会进入你自己的 iCloud 空间。

我们收集什么

我们不收集任何个人数据。App 不接入广告、分析或崩溃上报 SDK，不把录音、文本、标签、任务或设备标识上传到开发者服务器；也不具备读取系统「语音备忘录」数据库的能力，导入只能由你通过系统分享面板或文件选择器发起。

数据保存在哪里

数据位置
录音（.m4a）App 沙盒 Documents（可在「文件」App 查看）
笔记、转写、时间戳、标签、任务App 沙盒数据库
全文搜索索引App 沙盒支持目录（可重建）

删除笔记会同时删除音频与相关数据；卸载 App 会删除设备上的全部数据。

iCloud 私有同步（可选，买断包含）

默认关闭，只有在「设置 → iCloud 同步」里手动开启后才会生效。开启后，笔记文字同步到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库，录音放进你 iCloud Drive 里本 App 的 SpeakKeep 文件夹；两者都在你自己的 iCloud 账户中，开发者无法读取。关闭开关并重新打开 App 后停止同步，本机数据不受影响。换机时文字先到、音频按需下载。

权限

  • 麦克风：仅在你按下录音键时使用。
  • 语音识别：把录音转成文字；强制使用设备端识别，不回落到联网识别。
  • 相册（仅添加）：用于保存长图；未使用该功能时不会请求。

网络访问

App 只在两种情况下联网：初始化可选的本地转写模型（静态文件）；通过 StoreKit 完成购买与恢复购买。转写、搜索、标签与任务提取都在设备端完成，不需要网络。

儿童隐私

SpeakKeep 不收集任何人的数据（包括儿童），年龄分级 4+。
