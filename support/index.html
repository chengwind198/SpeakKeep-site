FAQ (English)

Why does a long recording need model initialization?

Short clips use the on-device speech recognizer. Longer recordings use a local long-form model that is initialized once and then runs fully offline. Initialization checks free space first and can be resumed.

Why does transcription slow down when the screen locks?

iOS limits background execution. Transcription is foreground-first and may pause when the app goes to the background. Keep SpeakKeep open, or connected to power, for long recordings.

What are the free limits?

Free: 10 minutes of transcription per day, up to 5 voice messages per day (recordings and imports combined), and up to 20 search results. The one-time purchase removes those limits and unlocks the larger local model and iCloud private sync.

My speech mixes two or more languages. What should I set?

On-device speech recognition works with a single language, so mixed-language speech can be misheard. Choose Auto Detect together with the initialized local model for better results. You can re-transcribe an existing note from the note’s ••• menu.

How does iCloud sync work?

iCloud private sync is optional (included with the one-time purchase) and off by default. When you turn it on, note text syncs to your own private iCloud database and recordings are stored in SpeakKeep’s folder in your iCloud Drive. The developer cannot read either. The setting takes effect after you reopen the app.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Settings → Lifetime Unlock and tap “Restore Purchases” while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I export audio?

On a note’s detail page, use the ••• menu to copy text, share a TXT, share the audio (.m4a), or export MP3. You can also find recordings in the Files app under SpeakKeep/Recordings (or in iCloud Drive when sync is enabled).

Why can’t SpeakKeep read the system Voice Memos library?

iOS does not provide access to another app’s recordings. Import them through the system share sheet or the Files app.

Deleting data

Deleting a note removes its audio and related data. Deleting the app removes all data stored on that device.

常见问题（简体中文）

为什么长语音要先初始化模型？

短语音用系统设备端识别；长语音使用本地长语音模型，初始化一次后完全离线运行。初始化前会检查剩余空间，中断后可继续。

锁屏后转写为什么变慢？

iOS 对后台执行有限制。转写以前台为主，切到后台可能暂停。处理长录音时建议保持 App 在前台，或连接电源。

免费版有哪些限制？

每天 10 分钟转写额度、每天最多 5 条语音消息（录音和导入合计）、搜索结果最多 20 条。买断后不限，并解锁更大的本地模型与 iCloud 私有同步。

我一句话里混说多种语言，该选什么？

系统识别是单语种，混说容易识别错。建议把转写语言设为「自动检测」并初始化本地模型；已有笔记可以在 ⋯ 菜单里「重新转写」。

iCloud 同步怎么工作？

iCloud 私有同步是可选的（买断包含），默认关闭。开启后笔记文字同步到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库，录音放在 iCloud Drive 里本 App 的文件夹中，开发者无法读取。设置需要重新打开 App 才生效。

换设备怎么恢复买断？

用同一个 Apple ID 登录后，在「设置 → 买断解锁」点「恢复购买」。

怎么导出音频？

详情页 ⋯ 菜单可以复制文本、分享 TXT、分享音频（.m4a）或导出 MP3；录音文件也可以在「文件」App 的 SpeakKeep/Recordings 找到（开启同步后在 iCloud Drive 中）。

为什么不能直接读取系统「语音备忘录」？

iOS 不开放读取其他 App 录音的接口，请通过系统分享面板或「文件」App 导入。

删除数据

删除笔记会同时删除其录音与相关数据；卸载 App 会删除设备上的全部数据。
