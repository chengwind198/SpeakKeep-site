Privacy Policy

NovaPlayer works without a NovaPlayer account. Your media is not uploaded to the developer.

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · applies to NovaPlayer 1.0 and later.

What we collect

The developer does not collect personal data through a NovaPlayer account or media relay service. NovaPlayer does not include developer-operated advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect your media files, viewing history, saved passwords or OAuth tokens.

Where your data is stored

DataLocation and destination
Media filesRemain on the local folder, NAS, media server or cloud drive you configure. Playback requests go directly to that source.
Library, progress, favorites and settingsStored locally. If you explicitly enable Pro sync, supported data is stored in your private iCloud database controlled by your Apple Account.
Server passwords and cloud OAuth tokensStored in Apple Keychain and sent only to the server or provider you configure.
Scan checkpoints and cloud cursorsStored locally as rebuildable data. They are excluded from iCloud backup and protected with file permissions.

Network requests

NovaPlayer makes network requests to sources you add, TMDB for metadata and artwork, Apple services used by the app, iCloud when private sync is enabled, and the App Store for purchases. TMDB requests may include media titles or identifiers but never include your media files.

Deleting data

Deleting a source in NovaPlayer also requests deletion of its Keychain credential. Uninstalling the app removes its local app-container database, but Apple does not guarantee that Keychain entries are removed. Remove sources in the app first if you want their credentials deleted. Private iCloud data can be removed through the app and Apple iCloud settings.

Tracking and children

NovaPlayer does not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking. The app does not collect data from anyone, including children, and is rated 4+.

Contact

Questions about this policy: visit the support page.

隐私政策（简体中文）

最后更新：2026-10-18，适用于 NovaPlayer 1.0 及以后版本。

NovaPlayer 无需注册 NovaPlayer 账号；开发者不会通过账号或媒体中继服务收集你的个人数据。

我们收集什么

NovaPlayer 不包含开发者运营的广告、分析或崩溃上报 SDK。我们不收集你的媒体文件、观看历史、保存的密码或 OAuth 令牌。

数据保存在哪里

数据位置与去向
媒体文件保留在你配置的本地文件夹、NAS、媒体服务器或云盘中；播放请求直接发往该来源。
媒体库、进度、收藏与设置默认保存在本机。只有你主动开启 Pro 同步后，受支持的数据才会进入由你的 Apple 账号控制的 iCloud 私有数据库。
服务器密码与云盘 OAuth 令牌保存在 Apple 钥匙串，只会发送给你配置的服务器或服务商。
扫描检查点与云盘 cursor作为可重建数据保存在本机，排除 iCloud 备份并受文件权限保护。

网络请求

NovaPlayer 会访问你添加的来源、用于元数据与海报的 TMDB、应用使用的 Apple 服务、开启私有同步后的 iCloud，以及 App Store。TMDB 请求可能包含影片标题或标识，但不会包含你的媒体文件。

删除数据

在应用中删除来源时，应用会同时请求删除对应的钥匙串凭据。卸载应用会移除 App 容器内的本地数据库，但 Apple 不保证卸载会清除钥匙串条目；如需删除凭据，请先在应用内删除来源。iCloud 私有数据可通过应用与 Apple iCloud 设置删除。

跟踪与儿童隐私

NovaPlayer 不将数据用于广告或跨 App 跟踪，不收集任何人的数据（包括儿童），年龄分级为 4+。
